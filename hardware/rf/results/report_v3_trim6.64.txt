## Notes (RF re-check 2026-10-05; v3 = case from the CAD's own solids, board with the pours restored)
What changed vs v2 (report_v2_trim6.64.txt):
- Board: route.py's keep-out lookup is fixed and the board re-routed: the F.Cu / In2 / B.Cu GND pours reach the antenna
  keep-out, so the feed is the 43.9 ohm GCPW again (sim.py records it, match.py uses it). Antenna, pads, RF chain and
  feed lengths are identical. As the v2 what-if predicted, the antenna barely notices (bare: X=0 2.982 -> 2.990 GHz,
  rad. eff. 0.84 -> 0.83).
- Case (spec.py + cad/horae.py, 2026-10-05): 6.80 mm tall (FLOOR 0.4, LIP 0.3, bezel gasket 0.15), plan corners R1.6,
  two PETG shells with a TPU 90A radial seal ring, bezel gasket and glass-corner cushions, full-height ears carried past
  the end faces and falling onto the bar boss, +X bar axis still 2.15 mm past the end face but now 1.95 mm above the
  floor (was 2.4), strap leaving at wrist level. sim.py now imports the CAD solids (case_stl.py -> case/*.stl, as
  polyhedra; TPU er 3.0 / tan d 0.05), so no hand-made case geometry is left to drift from the CAD.
- Effect: more PETG and TPU around the antenna end and the skin 0.1 mm closer: resonance -1.1 % in case (S11 minimum
  2.505 -> 2.478 GHz) and -1.6 % on the wrist (2.402 -> 2.362); rad. eff. in case 0.81 -> 0.80, on the wrist
  0.056 -> 0.047 (-0.8 dB).
Match: the fitted 0.3 pF / 1.2 nH still works but is now lopsided: in case -10.3 .. -14.2 dB, on the wrist -7.3 .. -12.1
  dB (the wrist's top band edge; 1.08 dB worst in-band transducer loss). Recommended: C11 0.5 pF C85920 (Murata
  GRM0335C1HR50BA01D, +-0.1 pF), L3 1.2 nH C77113 unchanged, C12 DNP: in case -8.8 .. -15.7 dB, wrist -8.8 .. -14.4 dB,
  0.81 dB worst. It beats 0.3 pF by ~0.3 dB of worst-case loss under every uncertainty tried (nominal 0.81 vs 1.08 dB,
  +-1.5 % resonance shift 1.21 vs 1.51, +-3 % 1.73 vs 2.01). 0.4 pF / 1.0 nH is equivalent (within 0.06 dB) but changes
  two parts. A one-part BOM change; if the BOM is frozen, 0.3 pF is acceptable and the bench tune decides.
Caveats as in v2: absolute resonance +-3 %, crude flat wrist slab (efficiency likely pessimistic), v2 bar sensitivity
  (-0.7 % resonance per mm closer) still applies.
