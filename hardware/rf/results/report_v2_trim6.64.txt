  C11 0.3 pF C76927 (Murata GRM0335C1HR30BA01D, +-0.1 pF) | L3 1.2 nH C77113 (Murata LQP03TN1N2B02D, +-0.1 nH) | C12 DNP
  The rev A values (0.9 pF / 0.6 nH) are now mistuned in the case: env -4.5 .. -8.4 dB in band (see above).
  Trim 6.14 (+0.5 mm: the 0.5 x 0.5 corner square over the open end, the most the board corner allows at the 0.3 mm
  edge clearance the +Y corner already has) was simulated: equivalent (minimax 0.69 vs 0.74 dB; robust minimax with
  +-3 % resonance shift 1.50 vs 1.57 dB; report_v2_trim6.14.txt), so not worth a footprint change. If the bench shows the
  case resonance high, `python3 meander.py 6.14` writes that footprint (results/SWRA117D_new.kicad_mod).
Efficiency (P_rad / P_accepted, 2.40 / 2.44 / 2.48 GHz): bare 0.81 / 0.84 / 0.87, case 0.80 / 0.81 / 0.83, wrist 0.056
  (-12.5 dB, body loss, flat in band); with the match (x transducer gain): case 0.69 / 0.74 / 0.77, wrist 0.05.
Spring bar (+X, steel, 2.15 mm past the end face): 1 mm closer moves the case resonance -0.7 % (S11 min 2.505 -> 2.487
  GHz; same at trim 6.14) and helps (case -10.8 .. -15.8 dB); no bar + strap moves it +1.1 % (2.533) and costs case
  -6.2 .. -10.4 dB. The bar is a parasitic radiator: it adds ~3 points of efficiency (0.78 -> 0.81). Keep BAR_DX within
  ~+-1 mm of 2.15 or re-run; a bar further out trends toward the no-bar case.
Model v2 (sim.py docstring): stack from spec.PCB_T (F.Cu 0.6 / In1 0.5 / In2 0.1 / B.Cu 0; 3313 prepreg 0.1, core
  0.4); ground = the board's real per-layer GND copper (as built only In1 reaches the keep-out, see below) + F.Cu GND
  stubs, C11/C12 GND pads, GND vias; IFA short = its 0.75 mm x 0.3 mm stub + via; antenna = meander.py trim of the
  untrimmed SWRA117D at the AE1 placement (self-checked against the board copper); case per spec.py + cad/horae.py
  (pebble rounds, lugs, +-X steel spring bars dia 1.5 x 16 mm at 2.15 mm past the end face and z 2.4, silicone strap
  loops er 3 / tan d 0.02, 3.7 mm loop + 1.8 mm x 12 mm strap; 3.0 mm cell = PEC); 32 mm of air before the PML.
  v1 (2026-10-03) had the PML ~4 mm off the case: harmless for env (env_v2_trim6.64_air4, PML 4 mm off everything: same
  Z within 0.1 ohm, same efficiency), but its wrist slab (60 x 50 mm) ran through the NF2FF box (x +-19.6) into the PML,
  so power absorbed outside the box counted as radiated: v1's on-wrist efficiency (0.27) was too optimistic.
  Mesh check env_v2_trim6.64_res0.07: S11 minimum 2.500 vs 2.505 GHz, Z within 1 ohm, efficiency 0.81: converged.
Board finding (route.py, not RF-owned): line 37 `ANT_KEEP_X = [... if z.GetIsRuleArea()][0]` returns the first rule
  area, which since fab1576 is a touch no-pour zone ('touch 3', left edge x = -7.5), so the F.Cu / In2 / B.Cu GND pours
  end at x = -7.5 and the stitching grid at x = -8.1 (HEAD has the same, ending at 'touch 1'). Only In1 reaches the
  antenna keep-out; the RF feed is a 46.5 ohm microstrip, not the 43.9 ohm GCPW the DRU rule assumes. Fix: select the
  zone named "antenna keep-out". RF impact of the fix is small: what-if geom_pour.json (extract.py --pour): X=0 2.548 ->
  2.550 GHz, rad. eff. 0.81 -> 0.81; the recommended match gives -8.6 .. -14.0 dB in case with the pours (GCPW, 0.1 nH
  cap grounds). No re-tune after the fix.
Caveats: absolute resonance +-3 % (PETG er 2.6 and print infill, e-paper stack as er 6 glass, FR4 er at 2.4 GHz,
  0.05 mm gaps meshed as 0.1, no solder mask / copper thickness). The env/wrist spread (~100 MHz)
  is the limit: a +3 % shift costs env ~4 dB of return loss at the band edge, -3 % costs wrist the same. Wrist = flat
  60 x 50 x 12 mm dry-skin slab (er 38, 1.46 S/m) directly under the floor: crude, likely pessimistic for efficiency.
  Verify on the first boards: VNA through a coax pigtail soldered to the C11 RF pad (feed trace to U1 cut) in the
  closed case, strap on, off and on a wrist; then retune C11/L3 (C12 is the spare shunt spot).
